Commercial Comps Field Office prototype
This page runs on your computer. Your sales file and share packs are files you open and save yourself. Nothing is uploaded.  There is no server behind it, and its security policy blocks every network request.

Your commercial sales and your network's, searchable in one file, ready for the report's comparison table.

Keep the sales your office confirms (inns, B&Bs, cottage resorts, apartments, retail, industrial, land, farms) with their NOI and cap rates and how each was confirmed. Open the share packs other firms send you. Then tick the sales you chose and copy them into your Excel or Word table.

You choose the sales. This page never ranks or suggests sales, and never computes a value, an adjustment or a cap rate for your subject. Figures it works out (price per unit, per sq ft, NOI ÷ price) show their arithmetic.

Share packs

For a network of firms that trade sales.
A share pack is a file. You send it to the other firms the way you send sales by email today, and you open theirs here. This page sends nothing.

Make a share pack

Only your own office's sales go in, each with who confirmed it and when. Sales other firms sent you are theirs to share, so they never go out again. Sales from an MLS listing never go out.

Not legal advice: check your board's MLS rules and CUSPAP before sharing.

Open a share pack

A sale you already have is not added twice. When the other firm's figures differ from yours, yours are kept and theirs are shown beside them, never written over yours. Their figures never go into your cap-rate summary unless the sale is new to you.
