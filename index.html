Field Office tools for appraisal offices

Each tool is one page that runs in your browser. It works on your own files, on your own computer.

Your data never comes here. These pages send nothing anywhere: their security policy blocks every network request. Files you open are read in your browser, and anything a page keeps stays in that browser on that computer.

  • Document ChaseRequest what a file is waiting on, by property type. See who to chase today, with follow-ups that list only what's still missing.
  • Comps library and gridKeep your office's comps searchable by town, date or a word like "solar", and lay the ones you pick out in report order.
  • Commercial compsYour sales and cap rates in one file, copied straight into your Excel or Word table, with share packs between firms.
  • Instant quote page: setupSet your own fees and build a quote page for your website. Clients see a price at once.
  • Inspection capture (early)Notes, rooms and the lender's photo list on your phone, laid out in report order back at the office.
